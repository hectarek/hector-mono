# Weeknight Chili

Serves 4. Takes 45 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- Salt, to taste

## Method

1. Brown the turkey in a large pot over medium-high heat, about 8 minutes.
2. Add the onion and garlic and cook until soft, 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the chili powder and cumin, then the tomatoes and beans.
4. Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring now and then. Season with salt.
